BetterHome

Apartment issue reporting and tracking platform

Welcome

BetterHome is a prototype designed to help tenants report apartment issues in a simple and organized way. Users can document problems like leaks, mold, pests, and broken appliances, then track those reports in one place.

This platform supports better communication between tenants and landlords by creating clearer records of maintenance requests and housing concerns.

Social Impact Goals

BetterHome was created with a social justice perspective in mind. Many tenants struggle to document housing problems clearly, and this can create power imbalances in communication with landlords or property managers.

Transparency

Creates a clear record of apartment issues and maintenance concerns.

Accountability

Helps both tenants and landlords track the status of reported problems.

Accessibility

Uses a simple layout and form structure so users can report issues more easily.
